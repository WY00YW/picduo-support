What to include

Include the steps, expected and actual results, and your app and iOS versions. View and choose to share diagnostics under Settings → Help & about → Feedback & support.

Photo not saved?

Check Photos permissions and free storage, then open Capture & save → Unsaved photos and the in-app album. Do not delete the app before confirming your photos are saved.

Buddy will not connect?

Keep both phones nearby with PicDuo in the foreground, and allow Bluetooth and local-network access. Compare the codes before continuing. If they differ or expire, cancel and search again. This beta requires clients supporting secure pairing; the legacy LAN fallback is unavailable.

Photos remain after disconnecting?

Shared albums retain content. Disconnecting stops the transfer, but received previews, downloaded originals and edits may remain. Manage deletion in the album; exported or saved copies on your Buddy’s device cannot be guaranteed to be recalled.

Sign-in and PLUS

Sign-in, subscriptions and purchases are not available in the first TestFlight release. The current test features require no payment.

Before sending feedback

Hide personal information in screenshots. Do not send passwords or verification codes. Email is sent only when you choose; this page does not automatically submit diagnostics or photos. You can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight.
