Privacy policy

Updated and effective: October 19, 2026 · iOS TestFlight

Scope and operator

This policy covers the first PicDuo iOS TestFlight release, operated by Yang Zhenwei (Will). Contact: picduo@163.com. This release provides photography, references, editing and nearby Buddy collaboration. Account sign-in, subscriptions and purchases are not available.

Photos and on-device processing

Camera frames, captured photos, references you choose to import and edits are processed and stored on your device. Portrait analysis supports on-device editing, does not identify you, and does not upload face features to our servers. Photos may retain metadata from the source file, such as capture time. Review images and metadata before sharing.

Nearby discovery and verification

When Buddy is on, PicDuo uses Bluetooth and the local network to find partners. Nearby devices can see the device name and a randomly generated app device identifier. Compare the verification code on both phones and continue only when the codes match exactly. The legacy unencrypted local-network fallback is disabled in this release.

What collaboration shares

After both people confirm a session, live preview, references, annotations, capture status and session photos can be shared with the current Buddy. Photos taken during collaboration can enter that Buddy’s shared album; the Buddy can view previews and download originals permitted by the album. Connecting does not open your entire system photo library. Photos travel between the devices over a verified, encrypted Nearby connection, not through a photo server operated by us.

Storage, disconnection and deletion

Ending a session stops transfer and clears temporary state such as live preview, but shared-album records, received previews, downloaded originals and edits may remain. Use the album’s remove, delete or permanent-delete controls as indicated. Revocations and deletions need a connection to synchronize. Copies already downloaded, exported, captured in screenshots or saved to Photos cannot be guaranteed to be recalled from the Buddy’s device. Forgetting a Buddy removes the pairing, not photos.

Photos, backups and local data

When auto-save is enabled or you save manually, photos are written to Photos and then follow your iCloud Photos and system backup settings. Recovery copies that have not been saved successfully can be saved or deleted under Capture & save → Unsaved photos; these recovery copies are excluded from system cloud backup. Other app data may be subject to system backup settings. Deleting the app removes data in its app container, not images in Photos. Pairing secrets are stored in the system Keychain; uninstalling does not guarantee Keychain deletion, so forget saved Buddies before uninstalling.

Preferences, permissions and choices

The app stores language, camera preferences, its device identifier, pairings and album records locally. Camera access supports capture; Photos access supports reference selection and saving; Bluetooth and local-network access support collaboration. Change permissions in iPhone Settings; photo access can be limited to selected images. Denying a permission affects the related feature, while features not requiring it remain available.

Third-party services and beta feedback

PicDuo uses Google’s open-source Nearby Connections component for device-to-device connections and Apple system services for capture, photo access and local processing. This release has no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics service. During TestFlight testing, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer, along with feedback you submit. We use this information to diagnose issues and improve the app. See the TestFlight privacy notice below for the data fields and Apple’s practices.

Support and diagnostics

We receive emails, diagnostic text and issue details that you choose to send. App-generated diagnostics include app and system versions and Buddy mode; you can add your issue description. They do not automatically include photos, accounts, device names or pairing secrets. Do not send Apple passwords, verification codes or unrelated personal information. We retain support and diagnostic information only as long as needed to handle issues and delete it when no longer needed. Copies on Apple’s platform follow Apple’s retention rules. Contact support to request deletion of information you sent us.

Access, correction and deletion requests

Manage local photos and references in the app or Photos, and withdraw permissions in Settings. For information you provided through support, email picduo@163.com to request access, correction or deletion. We will verify the request and handle it as applicable. Data stored only on your device must be managed on that device; we cannot remotely read or delete it. Minors should read this policy with a parent or guardian.

This website and policy updates

This website provides policy and support information without advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting service processes network information needed to serve pages, such as IP address, request time and browser information. Email links open your chosen mail service. If accounts, purchases or data practices change, we will update this policy and the in-app explanation and obtain authorization where required.
